# Opening cost evidence for linked brokerage accounts (Retired)

> **Status: Retired.** The in-review PDF/CSV opening-cost helper, transfer-cost
> PDF helper, and statement-to-Plaid sale matching screen under **Settings →
> Accounts → Bank and brokerage connections → Enable transaction history** have
> been removed.

## Current workflows

- **Statement-based account history (Merrill Edge and Charles Schwab PDFs):**
  Import monthly closing snapshots or reconstruct daily history directly from
  the account page. See
  [`docs/monthly-statement-history.md`](./monthly-statement-history.md) and
  [`docs/statement-daily-asset-history-plan.md`](./statement-daily-asset-history-plan.md).
- **Plaid brokerage transaction history:** **Enable transaction history**
  remains available under **Settings → Accounts → Bank and brokerage
  connections**. Enter verified opening unit costs and incoming-transfer unit
  costs manually during approval. Saved approvals, imported transactions, sync
  state, and live holdings sync are unchanged.
